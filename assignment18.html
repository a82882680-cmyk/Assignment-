How to measure yourself

  1. Chest: wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest.
  2. Length: measure from the top of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.
  3. Sleeve: measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the wrist.
  4. Shoulder: measure straight across from one shoulder edge to the other.
