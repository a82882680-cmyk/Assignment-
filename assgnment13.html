My Course: BCA in Artificial Intelligence

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications with a specialisation in Artificial Intelligence. My goal is to develop a strong foundation in programming, intelligent systems, and technology-driven problem-solving.

Course Learning Roadmap

  1. Programming Fundamentals: Learn programming concepts, variables, conditions, loops, and problem-solving.
  2. Web Development: Understand HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and website structure.
  3. Data Structures and Algorithms: Study methods for organising data and solving computational problems efficiently.
  4. Database Management: Learn how information is stored, organised, and retrieved.
  5. Artificial Intelligence: Explore intelligent systems, machine learning concepts, and AI applications.
  6. Cybersecurity: Develop an understanding of digital threats, network security, and threat detection.

Note: This is my general learning roadmap, not the university's official semester-wise syllabus.

Important Academic Terms

Artificial Intelligence (AI)
A field of computing focused on creating systems that perform tasks requiring abilities such as learning, reasoning, and decision-making.
Programming
The process of writing instructions that a computer can execute to perform specific tasks.
Database
An organised collection of information that can be stored, accessed, and managed efficiently.
Cybersecurity
The practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from digital threats.
Internship
A learning opportunity that helps students gain practical experience in a professional environment.
CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average, a measure of academic performance calculated according to an institution's grading system.
