Why each tag matters

  • charset - prevents garbled characters such as the rupee sign or accented letters.
  • viewport - makes pages usable on phones.
  • description - the snippet shown in Google results.
  • title - the name shown on the browser tab and in search results.
  • Open Graph - controls the preview when the link is shared.
